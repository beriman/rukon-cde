# Story 7.7: Meeting Management (Agenda & Minutes)

**Epic**: Epic 7 - Mobile AI Assistant  
**Story ID**: `story-7.7`  
**Story Points**: 5  
**Priority**: P1 (High)  
**Sprint**: Sprint 25 (Weeks 49-50)

## User Story

**As a** Project Manager,  
**I want to** manage meeting agendas and minutes within the platform,  
**So that** action items are tracked and not lost in emails.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Functional
- [ ] **Agenda**: Create meeting with Agenda Items
- [ ] **Minutes**: Record discussion points and decisions during meeting
- [ ] **Action Items**: Assign tasks to users with Due Dates
- [ ] **Indonesian Context**: Support "Notulen Rapat" format

### Integration
- [ ] **Calendar**: Sync with Google Calendar / Outlook

## Technical Tasks

### Frontend
- [ ] Meeting Editor (Rich Text)
- [ ] Action Item Tracker Component

## Dependencies
- **Depends on**: Epic 1 (User Mgmt)
