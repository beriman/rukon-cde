# Story 7.9: BCF-Linked Meeting Items

**Epic**: Epic 7 - Mobile AI Assistant  
**Story ID**: `story-7.9`  
**Story Points**: 5  
**Priority**: P1 (High)  
**Sprint**: Sprint 25 (Weeks 49-50)

## User Story

**As a** BIM Coordinator,  
**I want to** link meeting action items to BCF issues,  
**So that** we can view the 3D context of the problem during the meeting.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Functional
- [ ] **Link**: Attach BCF Issue to Meeting Item
- [ ] **View**: Click item to open 3D Viewer at specific Viewpoint
- [ ] **Sync**: Closing the Meeting Item prompts to close the BCF Issue (and vice versa)

## Technical Tasks

### Frontend
- [ ] Integrate BCF Selector in Meeting Editor

## Dependencies
- **Depends on**: Story 7.7 (Meeting), Epic 6 (BCF)
